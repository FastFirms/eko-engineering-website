The story

Why Eko exists.

Across Australia's commercial built environment and development sector, the same pattern shows up over and over: water and environmental engineering that doesn't connect to the people who have to use it.

Building owners receive audit reports they can't act on. Dust collecting reports that are long on findings, short on prioritised actions, calibrated to win the next engagement rather than solve the current problem.

Facility managers get water management plans written for the regulator rather than for the people running the building. These are so often defensible on paper, but useless on a Monday morning.

Construction crews work from environmental documents written by people who have never stood on a site at 6am, with crews ready to go. Never had to install a silt fence on a slope with no clear access, never had to explain a 47-condition consent to a site supervisor balancing a multi-staged project team.

Sustainability teams wrestle with monitoring data nobody has structured for the reporting frameworks they actually have to satisfy; NABERS, GRI 303, GRESB, AASB S2. Each one asking different questions of the same underlying numbers, and the data sitting in dashboards built for someone else's question.

These aren't failures of technical capability. The market has plenty of technically competent consultants. They're failures of orientation. The work was written for the wrong audience. The problem was never properly diagnosed.

Eko exists to close that gap. Providing solutions to identified problems, not what's easy to cut and paste.

The difference

Four things we do differently.

01

We orient every deliverable around the person who has to use it.

Every environmental report has two audiences the regulator and the person implementing it on site. Whether its site supervisors checking on crews in their ute, facility managers implement audit reports or sustainability managers providing recommendations to the board. Eko ensures regulatory compliance and maximum implementation success on every report that leaves our office.

02

We integrate water expertise across the whole practice.

Audits, management plans, water-related approval components, sampling, monitoring — all delivered by an engineer who writes water for a living, not as a sub-component of a generalist's broader report. Where water sits at the intersection of disciplines (and on most projects it does), there's one consistent technical author.

03

We coordinate, but we don't pretend.

The heritage, bushfire, flora and fauna, contamination, and arborist specialists every NSW DA needs are routed through a trusted external network. What we do in-house, we do well. What we don't, we route to people who do, and you have one accountable point of contact for the whole environmental package.

04

We're independent.

We don't sell technology, products, or rebates. We don't take commissions on equipment or referrals. Every recommendation we make is the one we think is right and you can read every report knowing nothing in it is shaped by a downstream sale.

Working with Eko

Working with Eko on your project.

For developers and builders

An environmental workstream that gets your DA shovel ready fast. Water-related impact assessments that anticipate the agency's questions rather than reacting to them. One accountable consultant for the whole approvals package. Compliance schedules your site team can use from the day consent is granted, not the week construction starts.

For commercial building owners

Audit findings prioritised by cost-benefit and fastest returns. Rather than alphabetised by chapter. Water management frameworks that survive the people who wrote them. A credible evidence base to take to your ownership group, body corporate, or sustainability committee.

For facility managers and sustainability teams

Structured visibility on where water actually goes in your property. Recommendations on sensors that move the most weight. Not monitoring just because you can. Monitoring data is validated and prepared for the reporting frameworks that matter to your organisation. Operational protocols for what to do when the system flags something, so you're never improvising under pressure.

For everyone

Fixed scope, fixed fee, one principal engineering firm accountable for the engagement. You always know what you're paying, what you're getting, and who to call.

Coverage

Servicing the Northern Territory, South East Queensland, and New South Wales.

Eko operates from two offices, offices in Varsity Lakes (Gold Coast) and Leanyer (Darwin). Our Darwin/NT presence is anchored in over ten years of Northern Territory water utility work and continues to service NT clients. The Gold Coast office services the SEQ region — Brisbane, Gold Coast, Sunshine Coast, Logan, Ipswich, Moreton Bay, Redland — as well as Northern NSW and Greater Sydney for the right project. Travel for site work is included in fixed-fee scoping for projects within those regions.

Note on scope: environmental approvals work is anchored in the NSW planning and biodiversity legislation framework. Queensland's planning system operates under different legislation, and while we deliver construction compliance and water services across both states, formal environmental approvals work is NSW-focused. We'll always tell you up front if your project sits outside our experience.
