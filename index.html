The Eko difference

Built for the people responsible on-site, with guaranteed approval.

Most consultants write reports purely for regulators. We write them to get you breaking ground fast, plus staying compliant. Written by people that have had to put up sediment fences in the rain.

First and foremost, on our mind is writing for the people who have to use these documents. Project managers running NSW development applications, site supervisors implementing compliance schedules, facility managers running NABERS Water programs, sustainability teams reporting against GRI and GRESB.

Every Eko deliverable is pressure-tested for implementation before it leaves our desk. Mitigation measures get checked against site reality. Conditions of consent get translated into compliance schedules people can follow without a law degree. Water audit findings get prioritised by cost-benefit, not page count. And the right approach gets assessed properly at the start. Saving you months by not lodging under the wrong instrument, designing a monitoring program for the wrong question, or building an action plan before the audit's done.

We're independent and technology agnostic. We don't limit you to one technology, product or latest tech craze. Every recommendation we make is the one we think is right, not the one that earns us a margin. And if conditions change, we adapt.

What we do

Three ways we keep your projects moving.

Environmental Approvals (NSW)

Get through NSW council and agency assessment without the back-and-forth that holds up your project.

We prepare Environmental Impact Statements, Reviews of Environmental Factors, Biodiversity Development Assessment Reports, Statements of Environmental Effects, and the water-related assessments that consistently attract the most scrutiny. We can act as the project manager for the full environmental workstream. Coordinating heritage, bushfire, flora and fauna, and arborist specialists through our trusted network so you have one accountable point of contact.

Construction & Compliance

Site documents written by someone who has stood on a construction site in the rain.

Erosion and Sediment Control Plans, Construction Water Management Plans, groundwater monitoring programs, and water sampling and analysis. Plans built for the rain, the program, and the crew you've actually got. Where ESCP and CWMP are both required, we prepare them as an integrated set.

Learn more

Water Services for Commercial Buildings

Water efficiency, monitoring, and management for commercial property owners, facility managers, and sustainability teams.

Non-revenue water management, smart meter integration, water audits and water management plans for utilities and commercial buildings. Drawing on the same nearly fifteen years of water engineering expertise that informs our development work. NABERS Water-ready. ESG-aligned. Audit-defensible.
