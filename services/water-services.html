The water side of Eko.

Water engineering is what our engineers cut their teeth on. Non-revenue water reduction, smart meter deployment, water efficiency programs and water management planning. That experience is what makes us credible on the water components of every approval and compliance document we now write.

It's also available standalone, for commercial building owners who want the same approach applied to their properties. We provide NABERS Water rating support including data preparation and management plan documentation.

Who we work with. Our water services are designed for commercial buildings, asset owners, strata committees, sustainability teams, utilities, local councils and facility managers. If you need to ensure you have a safe and secure water supply, even in drought conditions, we are here to help.

What's in this category

  • Non-Revenue Water Management — Find and reduce the water you're paying for but not using.
  • Smart Meter Integration & Data — Turn monitoring data into managed programs. The right sensors for the right application, integrated seamlessly into your existing operations.
  • Water Efficiency Audits — Evidence-based audits including IWA Water Balance and prioritised action plans.
  • Water Management Plans — A structured framework for managing water across your property, NABERS-ready.
