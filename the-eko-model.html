The Eko model

The right specialists, held to one standard.

Large consultancies carry the cost of a salaried bench whether your project needs those people or not. A loose group of subconsultants gives you the skills, but each one works to their own systems and answers to themselves. Eko is built to give you the first without the overhead, and the second without the risk.

The exact skills your project needs

We build each project team around the work, drawing on specialists who run their own focused practices. You get the person with the right expertise, not whoever happens to be free on the bench.

One system, whoever does the work

Every specialist on an Eko job works under Eko's quality, WHS and environmental systems, on Eko's project files, to Eko's reporting structure, level of detail and review checks. Whatever systems they use in their own practice, on your project they use ours.

One point of accountability

Eko holds the contract and answers for every deliverable. You deal with one lead, not a chain of separate consultants, and there's never a question about who is responsible.

Lean by design

No salaried bench waiting between projects, and no layers of internal meetings. Your fee goes into the work on your project, and decisions are made by the people doing it.

How we lead

Decentralised command, central accountability.

Eko runs on decentralised command: the lead sets a clear intent and standard, and the specialists closest to the problem lead their part of it. A good leader takes charge, and also knows when to follow someone who knows the subject better.

  • Clear intent.Every project starts with an agreed outcome, scope, standard and set of checks, set by the Eko lead.
  • Owned execution.Each specialist leads their part of the work and makes the calls in their field, without waiting on internal meetings.
  • Single accountability.Responsibility for getting the job right stays with Eko, whoever did the work.

How we resource projects

Five commitments on every engagement.

  1. A named backup for every lead.Every role on your project has a named, qualified alternate.
  2. Availability confirmed before we accept.We confirm in writing that the named people are available before accepting an order. If they're not, we name the backup or decline.
  3. We quote or decline every request.You get a clear answer on every RFQ, not silence.
  4. Independent review on every deliverable.Every deliverable has a named author and reviewer. If the usual reviewer is unavailable, we name an independent senior reviewer with your agreement, or we decline.
  5. Set escalation timeframes.On approval-pathway issues, you get verbal advice within one business day and written options within five business days.
